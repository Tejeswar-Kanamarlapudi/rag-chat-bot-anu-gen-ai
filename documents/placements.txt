The placement cell organizes campus recruitment activities for final-year students.

Students receive training in aptitude, logical reasoning, communication skills, programming, technical interviews, and HR interviews.

The placement process generally includes registration, aptitude assessment, technical assessment, technical interview, HR interview, and final selection.

The college also conducts resume-building workshops and mock interviews.

Students are encouraged to complete internships and industry projects to improve their practical skills.

The placement cell regularly invites companies from the software, information technology, consulting, and data analytics sectors.
